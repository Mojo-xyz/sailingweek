The model

Warm water. Steady wind. Serious fun.

Racing by day, food and music by night, and a destination that fills up because of it. The warm-water regatta is a proven format, and Somabay has the water, the wind and the venue to run it.

100–250

St. Maarten Heineken Regatta

Boats each year, sailors from 37 countries. Four days of racing by day and international acts by night. The largest warm-water regatta in the world.

1,500

Antigua Sailing Week

Participants from 24 countries, running since 1967. A full week, known as much for its parties as its racing.

130+

BVI Spring Regatta

Boats from 15 countries. A sailing festival wrapped around a regatta: hot racing, cool parties.

Sources: heinekenregatta.com, Antigua Sailing Week, BVI Spring Regatta. For scale, Europe's Kiel Week and Cowes Week run at 1,000–2,000 boats.
The Caribbean has a dozen of these. The Red Sea has none. That is the opportunity.
The venue

One destination. Every venue in play.

Somabay's marina is the hub of the week, with the beach clubs, hotels and restaurants around it as satellites, all linked by boardwalk and shuttle. Sailors, guests and partners never leave the destination.

  • The hub

    Somabay Marina

    The sailing village, the yacht showcase and the cruiser fleet, all on the dock.

  • The course

    Barbouni Beach Club

    Dinghy and windsurf racing close to shore, in full view of the beach.

  • Wind sports

    Wyte and The Kaktus Somabay

    Kite, wing and windsurf, demo sessions and the sports crowd.

  • The nights

    Across the resort

    A different venue every evening, from the opening party to the Cup.

The week

One week. Three things happening at once.

Red Sea Sailing Week is the umbrella, from arrival to closing party. Inside it: the racing, the public festival and a boat show. If it's raced, it's the Cup. If it's celebrated, it's the Festival.

Racing

The Cup

All classes, from cruising yachts offshore to dinghies and windsurfers in front of the beach. One overall trophy, awarded on the final night.

Public

The Festival

The Somabay Sailing Festival. A parade of wind sports, a sailing village, free demo sessions and a different party every night.

Marine industry

The Showcase

A boat show inside the week. Yachts in the water, brands on the dock and sea trials through the week.

Day 1

Arrival & opening

The passage fleet sails in from Egypt's marinas. Dinghies rig on the beach. Opening ceremony on the dock, then the first big night.

Day 2

Racing

Cruisers offshore. Dinghies and windsurfers race close to shore, in full view of the beach.

Day 3

Racing & demo day

Racing continues. Anyone can try sailing, wing or kite. Village and showcase open.

Day 4 · Peak

Festival Day

No scored racing. Every class on the water together. Families on the beach, music on the sand.

Day 5

Finals & the Cup

Short finals close to shore. Prizegiving and the closing party, the biggest night of the week.

Every day

The village

Food, vendors, talks, young sailors, brand activations and live music. It keeps the people who aren't racing here all week.

Every night

A different venue

Barbouni, The Kaktus, Wyte and the marina. The party moves each night, so the week tours the destination instead of staying in one bar.

Reference image
Day 4 · Festival Day

Everything on the water at once.

Sail, kite, wing and windsurf together in one parade. Cruisers processing under sail, the village at full strength, the whole destination out on the beach. The biggest crowd of the week, and the day with the most to photograph.

The festival

Food, music and the sea.

The Somabay Sailing Festival is the public face of the week. The racing is the backdrop. For most people, the nights are the event.

Nights

Nights that move

A different Somabay venue each evening. Opening party on the dock, closing party with the Cup.

Stage

Food and music

Local kitchens and a stage running from afternoon into the night. The kind of evening people come back for.

Beach

Come and try

Free demo sailing, wing and kite on the beach. The way in for anyone who has never been on the water.

Who comes

On the water, and on the beach.

Skippers, crews, yacht buyers and the marine trade on the water. Residents, hotel guests and families on shore.

25–30

Cruising yachts

El Gouna, Hurghada and Cairo skippers, plus yachts wintering in the Red Sea.

30–50

Dinghies & wind sports

Federation dinghy squads and the kite, wing and windsurf community.

New

Spectator fleet

Motor yachts and guest boats at anchor along the course, for close-up viewing and hosting.

Invited · Egypt

The home fleet

Sailing and yacht clubs from Cairo, Alexandria, El Gouna and Hurghada. Federation squads and junior teams. Brokers, dealers, marina and charter operators, marine trade and press.

Invited · International

The visiting fleet

Clubs and cruising associations from Turkey, Greece and Cyprus. Italian, French and Northern European sailing communities. Yacht brands, brokers, charter companies and sailing media.

The showcase

A boat show inside the week.

Red Sea Sails brings Hanse, Fountaine Pajot, Lagoon and X-Yachts, alongside FX Yachting's fleet and Felix Maritime as technical partner. Boats in the water, booths on the dock and sea trials through the week, in front of an audience that buys yachts.

2026 edition

Built on a proven first edition.

The Somabay Sailing Festival ran in May 2026. Yachts sailed down from El Gouna and Hurghada, dinghies and windsurfers raced off the beach, and the coverage travelled well beyond the event. Year two runs longer and bigger.
